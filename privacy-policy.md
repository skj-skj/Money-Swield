# Privacy Policy

**Money Swield**
Effective date: 15 October 2026

Money Swield is an offline expense tracker. It is built so that your data
stays on your phone and nobody else can see it.

## In short

- We do **not** collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
- There are **no accounts**, no sign-in, no servers and no cloud sync.
- There is **no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry**.
- Everything you enter stays **on your device**.

## Data you enter

Expenses, categories, events, payment methods, recurring items, photos and
settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. They
are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The app's own code makes no network requests.

## Permissions

The app asks only for what a feature needs, and only when you use it:

- **SMS (optional, off by default).** If you turn on SMS suggestions, the
  app reads bank debit messages on your phone, while the app is open, to
  suggest expenses. The message text is **never stored**: only the
  details needed for the suggestion (amount, payee, payment method, time,
  reference) and a fingerprint to avoid duplicates are kept, on your
  phone. Nothing leaves the device. You can turn this off at any time.
- **Camera and photos (optional).** Used only when you attach a picture
  to an expense. Pictures are kept in the app's private storage.

## On-device AI (Google Play version only)

The Google Play version can use Google's on-device AI (Gemini Nano,
through Google ML Kit and Android AICore) to read SMS suggestions more
accurately. This is optional and runs **on your phone**; your messages are
not sent to the developer. The AI model is downloaded by Android only
when you ask for it. Google's own components are covered by Google's
privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. The F-Droid / FOSS
version contains no Google code at all.

## Backups, reports and sharing

Backups, PDF and Excel reports, and saved photos are created only when you
ask for them, and are saved where you choose (for example your Download or
Pictures folder). If you share a file through another app, that app's
privacy policy applies to what you share.

## Deleting your data

All data lives on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data
in Android settings deletes it. Backup files and photos you saved outside
the app stay until you delete them.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue on the project's GitHub
page: <https://github.com/skj-skj/Money-Swield/issues>. Issues are public,
so don't include any personal or financial details in them.
