- First release on F-Droid.
- Add an expense in seconds: title, amount, category, Save.
- Totals by week, month and year, with filters and events.
- Reports with charts, exported to PDF or Excel.
- Recurring payments, photos of receipts, backup and restore.
- Optional suggestions from bank SMS, read only on your phone.
- F-Droid and GitHub get the exact same app, so you can install from
  one and update from the other.
